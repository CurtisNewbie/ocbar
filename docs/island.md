# The island surface and the card

The island's window, its per-screen behaviour and its expanded card, with the invariants a change to any of them could break.

The island is a third surface beside the menubar item and the bubble: a borderless, non-activating `NSPanel` at `NSWindow.Level(mainMenuWindow + 2)`, top-anchored, **one panel per attached screen** — `IslandHost` keeps one `IslandPanel` per `NSScreen.screens`, rebuilding the set on `didChangeScreenParametersNotification`, so only a change to the *set* of displays rebuilds and a removed display's panel is `retire()`d. The alert is deliberately **not** replayed onto a newly attached display: a rebuild retires the old panels, which folds them. On a screen with a notch the panel anchors to the **cutout**, not to screen centre (`auxiliaryTopLeftArea`/`auxiliaryTopRightArea` for its width, `safeAreaInsets.top` for its height); with no cutout it becomes a top-centred stand-in capsule ([`island-pill.md`](island-pill.md) owns its width and floor).

Hover grows the pill into a rounded card **500pt** wide (`islandExpandedCardTargetWidth`), sized so it truncates none of the real session titles, bounded to `0.8 × screen width`, floored at the capsule — sized by its *titles*, so widening it to restore an older ratio would only add black.

**The card's ground is opaque** (`islandCardFillAlpha` 1, the card's only fill, in `IslandCardView.draw`). That one number is the whole of the island's transparency: the panel is `isOpaque = false` with a clear background, no material and no shadow, so anything the wallpaper is allowed to show through, it shows through here. It shipped at α0.94, and that six percent was a real cost — a bright wallpaper shifted the card's tone and pulled the α0.5–0.7 text ladder under it down a step, on the built-in display, where the island is read most. The card stands in for the notch, and a notch has no wallpaper behind it. Nothing else on the surface is translucent at all — the cutout-less screen's 1px α0.08 edge is gone with the second shape below — so this one number really is the whole ground; the text and wash alphas are foreground, not ground.

**One shape on every display.** The card's own `draw` squares the top corners on all screens and rounds only the bottom (`cornerRadius` = `min(height/2, 14)`: 14 on a 32pt notched pill, 12 on the 24pt stand-in), and draws no border. It hangs from the screen's top edge either way, so a rounded top corner there buys nothing and, on a notched display, would leave black gaps beside the cutout. The stand-in capsule used to be the exception — a full stadium with a 1px α0.08 edge, because nothing forced it to merge with an edge — and that made the island change shape, not just size, when the pointer moved to the second display.

A row is **up to three stacked lines** — folder name + right-aligned age / title / context usage — and each absent line collapses the row, so `islandRowHeight(lines:)` = `30 + 14 × (lines − 1)` is one source of truth for the card's layout, the height budget and the window frame. The rule used to read "never two fields on one line"; line 1's right slot is its one registered exception, and it is not the case the rule was about. `5h36m · 2m` puts the age since creation beside the age since the last update, two fixed-width stamps rather than two texts, so neither can truncate the other — what the rule guarded against was a folder name and a title eating each other, which is why the title moved to its own line. The pair costs the folder name 34pt of its 384pt box — 48pt in the worst case, `· 1h6m` — and nothing else: no extra line, no extra height, and no extra rebuild, since both numbers are minute-granular and ride the same tick. The first keeps the α0.55 step it always had, the second takes α0.5 — one step dimmer, so the slot reads *how old, then how stale* rather than as one number with a stray space in it.

**The second stamp is `time.updated`, and it earns its place.** That timestamp was already being read to decide whether a session is recent enough to list, so the stamp costs no request; `SessionMonitor` now carries it as `SessionInfo.updatedAt`. It is shown only when the update is **at least a minute past creation** — a session nobody has touched since it started has `updated == created`, and `3h12m · 3h12m` would only say the age twice — and it renders `now`, never `0m`, for the first minute, because `0m` on a live session reads as a bug. It carries no `ago`: it sits inside the age's own sentence, one font, one unit, one right-aligned column of times. `ocbar.showSessionTime` gates it together with the age beside it — one toggle for every time on the card — and the dropdown's own rows are untouched, still showing only the created age.

**The card's top inset differs by state, and only the expanded side may move.** Collapsed `rowInset` 8; expanded `topInset` (the cutout's height) or 8, **plus `islandExpandedTopPadding` 8**, so the first row's *frame* clears the cutout's bottom edge and not just its text. The collapsed 8 must **not** move with it: 8 being *smaller* than the stand-in capsule's 24pt is what puts the collapsed rows inside the collapsed rect, which is what `hoverLatched` reasons about ([`island-alert.md`](island-alert.md)).

**The list is capped by a height budget, not a row count** (`IslandListBudget`): height = `contentTop` + rows + `actionRowHeight` + `bottomPadding` (+ `summaryRowHeight` when folded), where `bottomPadding` is the card's own **`cardBottomPadding`**: 8, plus another 8 (`bottomPaddingWithoutActionRow`) whenever there is no `Hide` row — the bottom mirrors the top's two steps, and an alert's card needs no third since its button already holds the row off the edge. The row budget is the smaller of 40% of the usable height and the room to the bottom of the usable area less that chrome — chrome never costs a visible row. The tail is an inert `+k more` row; folding is safe only because the list is re-ranked `error < waiting < busy < idle` (stably, in `attentionRanked`) with the first row always taken, so a fold can never hide an error behind an idle row — **except under an alert, where the fold deliberately hides everything but the one row that moved** ([`island-alert.md`](island-alert.md)).

`IslandCardView.hitTest` hands clicks only to session rows and — while an alert is armed — to the `Hide` button in its own frame; the summary is inert, and everything else (the card's margins included) falls through to the card's `onClick` toggle. **An armed alert makes that fall-through do nothing** ([`island-alert.md`](island-alert.md)). **A row click is answered by the host, not the panel:** `onRowActivated` → `IslandHost.rowActivated`, which folds **every** screen's island first, then calls `onSelectSession(pid)` → `SessionMonitor.focusSession`.

The card's third line is the dropdown's own readout, so the two surfaces cannot disagree — [`context-usage.md`](context-usage.md) owns that rule and the trap behind it.

**`AppDelegate.render` pushes `[IslandRow]` on every poll tick**, fanning one list to every screen's panel; the push hops to the main actor (`Task { @MainActor in }`) because `render` is nonisolated while the host and panels are `@MainActor`, and `menuWillOpen` calls the host's `collapse()`, collapsing **all** of them. Menu → "Show island" (`ocbar.island`, default on) toggles them, and the `Island hover delay` submenu beside it (`ocbar.islandHoverDelay`, default `100`ms, options 100–1000) sets the **open** dwell — read at schedule time so a pick applies to the next hover rather than the next launch. The **fold** is not in that menu: it is fixed at `hoverCloseDelay` 0.2s, because a pointer that has left an open card has already decided and should not wait out a dwell. With no rows (or disabled) every panel is out.

Four non-obvious requirements, each a real bug here: **hover is polled** — a 0.15s `NSEvent.mouseLocation` check against the *target* rect, **not** `NSTrackingArea`, whose `.inVisibleRect` area the window-resize animation tears down and rebuilds, synthesising enter/exit pairs while the pointer never moves (it oscillated the island forever); the expand/collapse delays are **one-shot** and guarded (`expandWork == nil` / `collapseWork == nil`), since the poll re-runs every 0.15s and a naive cancel-and-reschedule pushes the deadline back every tick (only the open's deadline is user-set — `ocbar.islandHoverDelay`; the fold's 0.2s is fixed — but both are *one-shot*, never per-tick); and the panel **must be keyable** (`canBecomeKey` true, `canBecomeMain` false) with every hit-testable view returning `acceptsFirstMouse == true` — a non-keyable non-activating panel of an inactive accessory app gets no mouse events at all, so the pill click, the row click and the `Hide` button are silently dead while hover still works; and **every frame change goes through the window's animator, the instant ones included** (`setExpanded(…, animated: false)` is a zero-duration animation, never a direct `setFrame`). A direct set does not cancel a frame animation already in flight — the animation writes its own target back on its next step — so a fold landing inside the 0.3s open (`collapse()` from a row click, the `Hide` button, or the menubar opening) put the surface back on the collapsed pill while the window kept growing to the **expanded** frame: a full-size black card with only the ring and the running count in the middle of it and no row anywhere. Starting an animation of any length is the only thing that cancels the previous one, and a zero-duration one applies the frame before it returns.
